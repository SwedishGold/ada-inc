## ✅ What WORKS (Based on Research)

### Content Types That Work
| Type | Engagement | Why |
|------|-----------|-----|
| **Document posts (PDF)** | 3x higher | Algorithm loves native content |
| **Text posts with hooks** | High | Easy to read, algorithms favor |
| **Personal stories** | High | Authentic, relatable |
| **Educational content** | High | Value for followers |
| **Questions** | High | Drives comments |

### Best Practices
- **3-5 posts/week** (start with 3)
- **Space ≥24 hours apart**
- **Engage within 60 min** of posting (reply to comments!)
- **Quality > Quantity**
- **Consistency** - LinkedIn algorithm rewards regular posters

---

## ❌ What DOESN'T Work

| Type | Problem |
|------|---------|
| External links | Algorithm penalizes |
| Spam/sales | People unfollow |
| Too frequent | Algorithm may shadowban |
| Generic content | No value, unfollows |
| Negative/troll | Bad for brand |

2. ✅ Be authentic & personal
