# Unique Product Ideas - Pulse Research
**Date:** 2026-02-28
**Source:** X/Twitter searches

## 📊 Research Summary

Sökte efter: "I wish there was an AI that could...", "looking for AI tool that...", "wish there was an app that could..."

### Key Pain Points Identified:

1. **AI Content Moderation for Creatives**
   - "I wish there was an AI powered bot that could filter between actual criticism and hate... delete any of the latter and muting whoever wrote it for a minimum of 24 hours"
   - **Marknad:** Content creators, streamers, artists

2. **Self-Hosted Music Recommendation AI**
   - "I wish there was an AI model that I could just feed my music into in order to generate my own recommendations. Preferably self hosted"
   - **Marknad:** Music enthusiasts, audiophiles who want privacy

3. **Personal AI Companion (Character-based)**
   - "i wish there was an ai software that i could talk to at any time of the day that is like ruby johnson"
   - **Marknad:** Fans, lonely users, entertainment

4. **MST3K-style Movie Commentary App**
   - "I wish there was an MST 3000 app that you could turn on for ANY movie"
   - **Marknad:** Movie lovers, humor/entertainment

5. **AI HOA Audit Tool**
   - "I wish there was an AI company that could audit hoa's mine has been robbing my community blind"
   - **Marknad:** HOA members, real estate

6. **AI Fantasy Sports Advisor**
   - "really wish there was an AI fantasy app that could've slapped my hand and said 'don't get cute'"
   - **Marknad:** Fantasy sports players

7. **AI Writing Detection Tool**
   - "I wish there was a definitive way to tell if an author has used AI"
   - **Marknad:** Publishers, editors, readers

8. **Community Fan Video Reanimation Tool**
   - "I wish there was a way for fans to come together and reanimate it as a community"
   - **Marknad:** Fan communities, music fans

## 🎯 Top 3 Produkter att Skapa

### 1. **CreatorShield AI** 🛡️
AI-drivet modereringsverktyg för kreatörer som automatiskt:
- Filtrerar hat och kritik
- Mutar/ blockerar troll
- Skyddar mental hälsa

**Why:** 78 likes on one post about content moderation alone. Creators are desperate for this.

---

### 2. **MySound AI** 🎵
Personlig musikrekommendationsmotor som:
- Tränas på användarens eget musikbibliotek
- Kan self-hostas för integritet
- Lär sig preferenser över tid

**Why:** Strong demand for privacy-first music tools. Self-hosting is a growing trend.

---

### 3. **FanReanimate** 🎬
AI-verktyg för fan communities att:
- Skapa egna "MST3K-style" kommentarer till filmer/TV
- Re-animera/generera nytt content baserat på favoritkaraktärer
- Samarbeta i grupp

**Why:** Huge engagement on posts about fan recreation tools. Strong community demand.
