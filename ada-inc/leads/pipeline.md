# Ada Inc. Sales Pipeline

## Flöde
```
🔍 Lead Hunting → 📧 Outreach → 💬 Follow-up → 💰 Close
     (Scout)          (Scout)        (Scout)       (CEO)
```

## Stage 1: Lead Hunting (Morning)
- Hitta potentiella kunder
- Sök: vård, tech, AI-intresserade företag
- Spara till: `leads/new-leads.md`

## Stage 2: Outreach (Afternoon)
- Skapa personliga mejl
- Lägg i Gmail-drafts
- Spara till: `leads/outreach-pending.md`

## Stage 3: Follow-up (Evening)
- Kolla tidigare outreach
- Skicka uppföljningar
- Uppdatera status

## Lead Status
- 🆕 New
- 📧 Contacted
- 💬 In Discussion
- 💰 Closed/Won
- ❌ Closed/Lost
