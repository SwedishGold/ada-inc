## 1. PromptBase - AI Prompt Marketplace

### Platform Overview
- **260,000+ prompts** for ChatGPT, Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Claude, Gemini, Veo, Sora, KLING
- **Platform fee:** 20% of sales
- **Seller payout:** 80% (via Stripe)

### Pricing Tiers (Standard Prompts)
| Price Point | Notes |
|-------------|-------|
| $1.99 - $4.99 | Entry-level, single prompts |
| $2.99 - $6.99 | Most common pricing tier |
| $5 - $10 | Premium prompts (established sellers) |
| Up to $20 | High-value specialized prompts |

### Key Insights
- **New seller cap:** $4.99 max until proven sales record
- Most prompts sell in the **$2.99 - $6.99 range**
- Video/Motion prompts (Sora, Veo) command **higher prices** ($6-20)
- Midjourney and image generation prompts are most popular

## 2. Gumroad - Digital Products Platform

### Platform Overview
- Sellers set their own prices
- **Platform fee:** 10% (standard) or 0% with Gumroad Pro
- More flexible than PromptBase

### Typical Price Points for AI Products

| Product Type | Price Range | Examples |
|--------------|-------------|----------|
| Single prompt pack (10-30 prompts) | $0.99 - $9.99 | $0.99 starter packs |
| Midjourney/Image prompts | $9.99 - $29.99 | Bundle of 8 packs: $29.99 |
| Comprehensive prompt collections | $19 - $49 | Ultimate bundles |
| AI courses/tutorials | $29 - $197 | Full courses |
| Templates + Prompts combo | $15 - $39 | Notion + prompts |

### Real Examples ( Salesfrom Gumroad)
- "20 AI Prompts for Passive Income" → **$7-20** (pay-what-you-want option)
- "2,500+ ChatGPT Prompt Templates" → **$19+**
- "8 Premium Prompt Packs Bundle" → **$29.99**
- "Ultimate Prompt Collection Vol 1-7" → **$29.99** (full bundle)

### Successful Pricing Strategies
- **Low-tier entry:** $0.99-4.99 to build audience
- **Mid-tier:** $9.99-19.99 for quality packs
- **Premium bundles:** $29-49 for comprehensive collections
- Pay-what-you-want can increase average sale price

## 3. Etsy - Digital Products (Caution!)

### ⚠️ Important Policy Update (2025-2026)
**Etsy now PROHIBITS selling AI prompt bundles.**  
The platform views prompts as "an integral part of the creative process."

### What's Still Allowed
- AI-generated **printables** (generated using AI, but the OUTPUT is the product)
- AI-assisted **design templates** ( Canva templates, planners)
- Digital products where AI is a tool, not the product itself

### Pricing (for permitted products)
- **Simple digital downloads:** $2-5
- **Templates/Planners:** $5-15
- **Comprehensive bundles:** $10-25
- **Premium printable sets:** $12-20

## Market Standards Summary

### Recommended Pricing for AI Prompt Products

| Product Type | Budget (Entry) | Standard | Premium |
|--------------|----------------|----------|---------|
| Single prompt | $2.99 | $4.99 | $6.99 |
| 10-30 prompt pack | $4.99 | $9.99 | $14.99 |
| 50+ prompt pack / Bundle | $9.99 | $19.99 | $29.99 |
| Full suite / Ultimate | $19.99 | $39.99 | $49.99 |

### Key Takeaways

1. **PromptBase** is the premium marketplace - higher buyer expectations, use established pricing
2. **Gumroad** offers more flexibility and higher potential margins (lower fees)
3. **Avoid Etsy** for prompt bundles - focus on AI-generated outputs instead
4. **Video/Image generation prompts** (Sora, Midjourney, Veo) command 2-3x text prompts
5. **Bundling** is key - collections sell better than single prompts at 2-3x the price
